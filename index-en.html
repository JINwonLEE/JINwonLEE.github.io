About

For about eight years I have been taking agent applications, cloud platforms, and user-facing services into production. At Samsung Electronics I design and build an internal AX Agent Platform on AWS EKS, and I supported a global B2C generative AI service on Azure through its UK rollout and early stabilization.

Before that I led production delivery and application reliability for a company-wide AI chatbot used by 30,000 employees at Samsung Research, built Kubernetes platforms and a Linux packet-mirroring application measured at up to 130,000 packets per second in Samsung's Network Division, and worked on an OS-level AI assistant at TmaxOS.

I started in research: my master's work at UNIST on distributed CNN training reported up to a 2.3× speedup. Outside work I am building OfferNow, a job recommendation service that shows the CV evidence behind every match.
